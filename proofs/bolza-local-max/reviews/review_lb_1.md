## Overall verdict
**ACCEPT** (with the three cosmetic fixes F1–F3, none of which changes a number).

I tried hardest to break the shift and indexing step (the zero eigenvalue) and the cross-gluing H(div) conformity. Both hold, by
hand derivation, by a second literature source written exactly in shifted form (BMPW Thm 2.1), by an exact toy model in which the
wrong indexing demonstrably fails, and by an independent geometric gluing check. The certified number
λ₁ ≥ 3.83887570105272435… is reproduced independently from the stored Arb matrices in mpmath interval arithmetic. The element
integrals are confirmed to 1e-32 against a from-scratch metric. The one irreducible trust input is ρ = 5 ⇐ λ₄ > 4.0 (Lemma 1(a), LP).
The computation cannot detect a wrong ρ (ρ = 7.5, truly invalid, still returns a sub-true value), so the LP certificate is
load-bearing and is the right thing to cite.
